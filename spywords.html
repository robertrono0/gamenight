🕵️‍♂️ Spy Words

Uses 2 teams. Each team needs a spymaster with a phone (they see the secret key).

📜 How to Play

🟥🟦Two teams. Each has a spymaster who sees the secret key on their phone.
🗣️The spymaster says ONE word and a number, like "Ocean, 2", to point at their team's words.
👆The team talks it over and the host taps their guesses on the TV, one at a time.
⬜Hit a neutral word or the other team's word and your turn ends.
💣Hit the BOMB and your team loses instantly!
🏆First team to find all their words wins.
