📜 How to Play

🙈One player is the guesser. Everyone else sees a secret word on their phone.
☝️Each helper types ONE word as a clue.
❌Matching clues cancel out! If two people write "cheese", nobody sees it. Be creative!
📺The TV shows the surviving clues. The guesser types one guess on their phone.
🤝It's co-op: the whole room tries to get as many right as possible.
💯Correct: guesser +100, each unique clue +50.
