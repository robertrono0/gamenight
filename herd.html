📜 How to Play

❓Everyone gets a simple question like "Name a breakfast food".
🐑Type the answer you think most people will type. Don't be original!
✅Everyone in the biggest group gets a point.
🐮If you're the only one with a unique answer, you get the Pink Cow. You can't win while you hold it!
🔀Host: tap two answer groups to merge them (like "fries" and "french fries").
🏆First to the target score without the cow wins!
