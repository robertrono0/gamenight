🎮 The games

🕵️

Who's the Imposter?

Everyone scans one QR code. One (or more) of you is secretly the imposter.

~15 min · warm-up
🎱

Gobble Bingo

Cardboard cards, prize rounds, $100 split across the games. Auto-call is on the ▶ button.

~30-40 min
🐝

Spelling Bee

Classic or tournament heats, lifelines, harder words each round, champion podium.

~30-45 min
🦃

Pluck the Turkey

Hangman, Thanksgiving style. Wrong letters pluck a feather. Lose them all and the turkey is NAKED 🫣

~10-20 min · filler
💰

Jeopardy!

Teams, solo or tournament. Daily Doubles, Final Wagers and a podium at the end.

~60-90 min · the finale

🗓️ Suggested running order

Arrival
🕵️ ImposterEasy icebreaker while people arrive and grab food. A few quick rounds.
Dinner
🎱 BingoWorks while people eat. Turn on ▶ Auto so you can eat too. It pauses by itself when someone yells BINGO.
After
🐝 Spelling BeeEveryone's full and sitting down. Classic for under 6 people, Tournament for more.
Dessert
🦃 Pluck the TurkeyQuick and silly while pie is served. Great warm-up before the Jeopardy finale.
Finale
💰 Jeopardy!Save the best for last. Teams mixed every round is the default. Tournament if everyone wants a champion.

✅ Before guests arrive

💡 Host tips

Made a mistake? Every game has ↩️ Undo. Jeopardy's is in the ☰ menu.
Refreshed by accident? No worries. Every game saves itself and picks up where you left off.
Jeopardy reading rule: nobody answers until you finish reading the clue. The clock waits for you.
Full screen looks best on a TV: tap ⛶ in any game.
