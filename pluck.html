📜 How to Play

🧩A secret word or phrase is hidden on the board. The category is your clue.
🎯On your turn, call out one letter. The host taps it.
✅Letter is in it? +100 for every time it shows up, and you go again!
💥Letter not in it? You lose a piece: a balloon pops, the hangman gets drawn, the snowman melts or someone eats a pizza slice. Then it's the next player's turn.
💡Think you know it? Solve it! Right = +300 bonus + 100 for every hidden letter. Wrong = you lose a piece.
⏱10 seconds to call a letter or you lose your turn! Get 3 right in a row and you're 🔥 ON FIRE: letters pay 2×.
🌟A golden letter hides in every puzzle (3×), and one puzzle is a 💰 DOUBLE MONEY puzzle!
💸Careful! A wrong letter costs $100, a wrong solve costs $300, and the hidden 💀 bad apple letter costs $250. You can go negative!
🎈Lose all 7 pieces and nobody gets the bonus. Most money at the end wins!
