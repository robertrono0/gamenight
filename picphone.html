📜 How to Play

✍️Everyone writes a secret phrase (or gets a random one).
🎨Your phrase goes to the next person, who has to draw it.
🤔The drawing goes to the next person, who guesses what it is.
🔁Draw, guess, draw, guess... the message gets more and more scrambled!
📺At the end, the TV reveals every chain step by step. Prepare to laugh. 😂
🗳️Vote for the funniest chain. Everyone in that chain gets +100.
