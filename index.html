🚀 First time hosting? Start here

1. Open on the TVOpen this site on the laptop connected to the TV (or cast/screen-share the tab). Tap ⛶ in any game for full screen.
2. Check everyone inGuests scan the big QR above, type their name and make a character. They show up on the wall.
3. Pick a gameTap 🎯 What should we play? or build a 🎬 playlist. Every game explains itself with a ❓ How to Play screen.
4. Pick a host phoneIn a game, tap 📱 (bottom right), then tap your name to become 🎤 host. Your phone runs the show.
5. CelebratePoints go to the leaderboard by themselves. End with 🥁 Reveal the standings and the 📜 Night recap.

💡 Host tips

Made a mistake? Every game has ↩️ Undo (Ctrl+Z). Jeopardy's is in the ☰ menu.
Refreshed by accident? No worries. Games save themselves and pick up where you left off.
Phones not connecting? Same Wi-Fi as the TV. A backup panel appears so you can tap who buzzed.
Grandparents playing? Turn on 🔠 Big text. Phones have an ع button for Arabic.
Kids around? Turn on 🧸 Kid mode. Spicy prompts stay off unless you turn on 🌶️.
Slow or laggy laptop? Turn off the 3D lights and floating sparkles for every game.
