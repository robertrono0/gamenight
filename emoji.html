How many puzzles?

🔔 Players buzz in on Multibuzzer. First to buzz gets to answer.

📜 How to Play

😎Emojis appear on the TV. They spell out a movie, song, food, show or saying.
🔔First to buzz gets to answer out loud.
💯Right answer early = 300 points. After the letter hint drops it's worth 150.
❌Wrong answer? That team is locked out and someone else can buzz.
⏱️30 seconds per puzzle. The hint shows up at 15.
🏆Most points after all the puzzles wins.
