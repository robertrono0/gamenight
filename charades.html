📜 How to Play

👥Split into teams. Each turn one player is the clue giver and faces the TV.
🙈Their teammates turn their backs to the TV and shout guesses.
🎭The card says how: ACT IT (no talking!), DESCRIBE IT (don't say the word) or HUM IT.
✅Host taps GOT IT (or → key) for every correct guess. SKIP (← key) if stuck. No penalty.
🔥3 in a row = ON FIRE, and every card after that is worth 2!
🏆Teams rotate and clue givers rotate. Most points after all turns wins.
