📜 How to Play

🛍️An item shows up. Each team huddles and agrees on one price guess.
⌨️The host types every team's guess, then hits REVEAL.
🎯Closest without going over wins 100 points.
💥Everyone over? Then the closest guess still wins, but only 50.
🤑Within $1 (or 1%) = BULLSEYE for 300 points!
💡Prices are approximate US retail prices.
