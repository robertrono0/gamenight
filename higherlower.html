Cards

📜 How to Play

🃏The TV shows one thing with a number, like Eiffel Tower: 330 m tall.
🤔Then a second thing appears. Is its number HIGHER or LOWER?
📱Everyone votes on their phone.
✅Right = +100, plus +50 for every answer in a row you get right (streak!).
❌Wrong resets your streak.
🏆Most points wins. Numbers are approximate.
