📜 How to Play Family Feud

🙋Face-off: one player from each family comes up. First to buzz (use your buzzer site) answers.

⌨️Players take turns answering. The host types what they say, and the game finds it on the board.
❌Not on the board = a strike. Three strikes and you're out!
🕵️After 3 strikes the other family huddles and gets one guess to STEAL all the points!
💰Round 4 is double and the last round is triple. Most points wins!
