# Traitors (vertical slice)

## Player fantasy
"I'm on the show." Secret role on your phone, dramatic breakfast reveals, a tense round table, and the thrill of lying to your family's face (or catching them).

## Core loop (one day, about 10 to 15 minutes)
1. Night: everyone taps on their phone. Traitors choose a victim (or recruit); Faithful make a decoy "suspect" tap so nobody stands out.
2. Breakfast: players walk in one by one on the TV; the missing person is revealed.
3. Mission: alternates between phone-played challenges (Trivia Sprint, The Vault, One Mind) and talk challenges. Money goes into the pot.
4. Secret prizes: best mission players secretly win the shield, revive potion (Faithful only), the dagger (double vote), and late game the Seer.
5. Round Table: timed discussion, phone vote, dramatic vote reading, the banished player stands and declares, then the TV reveals the truth.
6. Endgame at 4 or fewer: unanimous vote to end, or banish again.

## Rules
- Classic by default (no Doctor/Detective). Mafia twist optional.
- Traitors are never revived. Recruiting: if a Traitor is banished, remaining Traitors may recruit; a refused offer means death.
- Win: all Traitors gone (Faithful split the pot) or Traitors equal or outnumber Faithful (they steal it).

## Pacing targets
Night under 60 seconds. Breakfast reveal under 20 seconds. Mission 60 to 90 seconds. Round table 2 to 5 minutes (host setting).

## Done looks like
- A full game runs with TV as the host and zero laptop clicks.
- Every phone screen during night looks identical from across the room.
- Reload mid-game recovers.
- Live-tested with 4 or more real phones.
