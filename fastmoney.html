💸 Set up Fast Money

Two players answer 5 survey questions against the clock. Together they need 200 points to win!

Player 1

Player 2 (send them out of the room while Player 1 plays!)

📜 How to Play

🚪Player 2 leaves the room (or wears headphones!).
⏱️Player 1 gets 20 seconds for 5 survey questions. The host types each answer and hits Enter. "Pass" to come back later.
📋Answers are revealed one by one with their points. The host can tap any answer to fix the match.
🙈Player 1's answers are covered. Player 2 comes back and gets 25 seconds.
🔁Same answer as Player 1? BZZT! "Try again!"
🏆Reach 200 points together and you WIN! Host tip: type answers on the 🎤 host phone.
