🗡️ Set up The Traitors

Everyone needs their phone. Best with 6 to 20 players. The host's phone (🎤) secretly sees every role.

Round table discussion time

📜 How to Play

🎭Phones secretly make a few players Traitors. Everyone else is Faithful. Traitors know each other.
🌙Night: Traitors secretly murder one Faithful on their phones. Everyone taps something at night so nobody gets caught by their phone.
☀️Breakfast: players "arrive" one by one on the TV. Who didn't make it?
💰Mission: the group does a challenge together to add money to the prize pot. Winners may earn a shield.
🔥Round table: argue, accuse, then everyone votes on their phone to banish someone. Their role is revealed!
🏆Banish every Traitor and the Faithful win. If Traitors make it to the end (or equal the Faithful), they steal the pot!
