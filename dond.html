💼 Set up Deal or No Deal

Who's playing? (everyone at the party, one per line)

Game size

How many contestants?

Contestants are picked at random. Everyone else is the audience: yell DEAL or NO DEAL! Tap 📱 (bottom right) so the host's phone becomes the 🕴️ Banker, who secretly knows what's in the contestant's case.

📜 How to Play

💼Every case hides a money amount, from 1 cent to $1,000,000 (Quick: $500,000). Nobody knows which is where.
👉The contestant picks ONE case to keep. It stays closed until the very end.
🔓Then they open the other cases, a few each round. Every amount revealed is NOT in their case. Big amounts gone = bad news!
📞After each round, the Banker calls with an offer to buy their case.
🤝DEAL: take the money and the game ends. NO DEAL: keep opening. The audience yells advice!
🏆Last 2 cases? Keep or swap! Biggest winner of the night tops the podium.
