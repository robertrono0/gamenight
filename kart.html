📜 How to Play

📱Your phone is your steering wheel: hold ◀ or ▶ to steer. Your kart drives forward by itself!
💨Hold DRIFT while turning to slide around corners. Let go for a speed boost!
🛹Hit a ramp and tap DRIFT in the air for a trick boost.
❓Drive through ? boxes for items, then tap the item button: ⚡ boost, 🛡️ shield, 🛢️ oil slick, 🌶️ pepper rocket.
🏁Most laps first wins! Off the road you slow down.
🏆1st to 8th all score points for the Game Night leaderboard.
