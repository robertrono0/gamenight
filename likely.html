📜 How to Play

👉Most Likely To: read the card, hit countdown, and on POINT! everyone points at someone at the same time.
👑Host taps whoever got the most fingers. They win that title. Ties? Tap both.
🗣️The winner gets 10 seconds to defend themselves. Or not. 😂
🤔Would You Rather: on GO! everyone runs to the LEFT or RIGHT side of the room.
⚖️Host taps the side with more people. The smaller side has to explain themselves!
🏆At the end, everyone gets their trophies and we crown the MVP.
