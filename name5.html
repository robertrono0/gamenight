📜 How to Play

👥Split into teams. One player from the team goes up each turn.
📢A category appears, like "5 things in a fridge".
⏱️Shout out 5 answers in 10 seconds!
✋Host taps how many they got (0 to 5), or presses 0 to 5 on the keyboard.
⭐All 5 = +2 bonus.
🏆Team with the most points wins.
