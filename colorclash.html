🃏 Color Clash

Mode

Turn timer

Jump-in (play an identical card out of turn)

Cards are dealt to everyone's phone. Extra decks are added automatically for big groups. The game ends when 3 players run out of cards.

📜 How to Play

🃏Everyone gets 7 cards on their phone. Match the top card by color or number/symbol.
⊘ ⇄ +2Skip the next player, Reverse direction, +2 makes the next player draw 2.
🌈Wild picks a new color. Wild +4 also makes the next player draw 4.
🗣️Down to 2 cards? Tap LAST CARD! before you play, or draw 2 as a penalty.
⚡Jump-in: have the exact same card as the top one? Play it any time, even out of turn!
🏆First 3 players to empty their hand win. Each mode adds its own twist (see the mode list).
