📜 How to Play

🧩A secret word or phrase is hidden on the board. The category is your clue.
🎯On your turn, call out one letter. The host taps it.
✅Letter is in it? +100 for every time it shows up, and you go again!
🪶Letter not in it? The host plucks a feather off the turkey and it's the next player's turn.
💡Think you know it? Solve it! Right = +300 bonus + 100 for every hidden letter. Wrong = lose a feather.
⏱10 seconds to call a letter or you lose your turn! Get 3 right in a row and you're 🔥 ON FIRE: letters pay 2×.
🌟A golden letter hides in every puzzle (3×), and one puzzle is a 💰 DOUBLE MONEY puzzle!
🦃All 7 feathers gone? the turkey is NAKED 🫣 and nobody gets the bonus. Most money at the end wins!
