📜 How to Play

🎯Each round, one player is in the hot seat.
📶They secretly rank 5 things on their phone, like "best pizza topping", from favorite to least favorite.
🤔Everyone else guesses their #1 and their #5.
✅+100 for each correct guess.
🎤The TV reveals their ranking from #5 up to #1. Defend your choices!
🏆Most points wins. Find out who really knows each other.
