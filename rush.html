📜 How to Play

🔤A random letter and 6 categories appear.
📱On your phone, write an answer for each category that starts with that letter.
🚫If someone else wrote the same answer, it doesn't count for either of you!
✅+1 for every unique, valid answer.
👆Host: tap any answer on the TV to throw it out (or bring it back).
🏆Most points after all rounds wins.
