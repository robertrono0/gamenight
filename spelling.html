Tonight's rules 🎬

📵 No phones. The studio audience can't help!
🎤 The host reads your word. Like a real bee, you can ask to hear it again, or for the definition, part of speech, language of origin, or a sentence.
✅ Spell it right and you climb the ladder.
❌ Spell it wrong and you're out at the end of the round.
😂 If EVERYONE left misses in the same round, nobody's out.
⏱️ 30 seconds on the clock. No take-backs once a letter is said.
🏆 Tournament nights: groups of 3-4 play their own bee (a heat). Each heat winner goes to the 👑 Championship for harder words. Win that and you are the champion!
🛟 Lifelines, once per player per game: 🔤 First Letter (see how it starts), 📞 Phone a Friend (someone whispers, +10 sec) and 🔁 Second Chance (your first miss is forgiven, you get a new word). No lifelines in the Sting-Off!
