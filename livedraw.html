📜 How to Play

✏️One player draws a secret word on their phone. It shows up live on the TV!
⌨️Everyone else types guesses on their phone, as many as they want.
⚡Faster correct guesses score more (up to +200).
🎨The artist gets +50 for every person who guesses it.
💡Letters of the word are revealed as time runs out.
🚫No letters or numbers in your drawing!
