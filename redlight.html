📜 How to Play

🏃Tap the big button on your phone as fast as you can to run toward the finish line.
🟢Only move on a GREEN LIGHT.
🔴If you tap during a RED LIGHT, you're OUT! 💥
⚠️The light can change at any moment. Listen for the sound!
🏁First 3 across the line score 300 / 200 / 100. Everyone else who finishes gets 50.
👥Works with 30+ players. Great for kids and grandparents too!
