🧠 Mind Meld

Rounds per team

📜 How to Play

🧠A dial runs between two ideas, like COLD ⟷ HOT. A secret target is hidden somewhere on it.
📱One player (the psychic) sees the target on their phone and gives a one-phrase clue, like "soup".
🎚️Their team discusses, then the host moves the needle on the TV (← → keys or drag).
🎯Bullseye = 4, close = 3, near = 2.
↔️The other team guesses if the target is further left or right for +1.
🏆Most points wins!
