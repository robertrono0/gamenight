📜 How to Play

🎡Spin the wheel, then call a consonant. Every time it's in the puzzle, you earn that amount.
🅰️Buy a vowel for $250 (A E I O U). Vowels don't earn money.
💡Solve any time it's your turn. Right answer = you bank your money for the round (at least $1,000).
💀BANKRUPT wipes your round money. LOSE A TURN passes to the next team.
❌Miss a letter, call a used letter, or solve wrong and the turn passes.
🎁The leading team plays the BONUS ROUND: R S T L N E are free, pick 3 more consonants + 1 vowel, then 10 seconds to solve!

Host tip: type letters on the laptop keyboard, or tap 🎤 Host remote (bottom right) to see the answer and run the game from your phone.
