📜 How to Play

📱Everyone gets the same prompt on their phone, like "The worst thing to hear at a wedding: ___".
✍️Write the funniest answer you can. Answers show up on the TV anonymously.
🗳️Everyone votes for their favorite. You can't vote for your own!
💯+100 for every vote you get. The crowd favorite gets a +100 bonus.
🏆Most points after all rounds wins.
🌶️Spicy mode adds cheeky adult prompts. Keep it clean with family!
