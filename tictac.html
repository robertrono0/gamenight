🦃 Set up Tic-Tac-Turkey

This one is 2 teams: 🦃 Turkeys vs 🥧 Pies.

Match length

📜 How to Play

🎯Two teams: 🦃 Turkeys and 🥧 Pies. Each square on the board is a category.
👆On your turn, pick a square. The host reads the question. Your team talks it over and answers.
✅Right? You claim the square with your mark.
🕵️Wrong? The other team can steal it with the right answer.
❌⭕Get 3 in a row to win the board. Board full with no winner? New board!
🏆Win the most boards to win the match.
