📜 How to Play

🗣️The TV chants 5 words in a loop: PIZZA, DOG, DONUT, DUCK, TACO... and flips a picture card each time.
🖐️When the picture matches the word being said, everyone SLAPS the big button on their phone!
🐢The last one to slap gets a penalty card. Missed it entirely? Penalty too!
🚫Slap when it's not a match? Penalty!
🃏Fewest penalty cards at the end wins.
🔊Turn the sound up. Chanting along out loud is encouraged!
