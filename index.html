🏆 The big shows

💰

Jeopardy!

Teams, solo or tournament. 43 categories, Daily Doubles, Final Wagers and a podium.

~45-90 min · the finale
🗣️

Family Feud

Two families face off. Type what people say and the board finds the answer. 35 surveys.

~15-35 min · 2 teams
🐝

Spelling Bee

Starts easy and gets harder. Lifelines, tournament heats and a champion podium.

~20-40 min

🎉 Party games (any group size)

🕵️

Who's the Imposter?

One QR code, everyone gets a secret word except the imposter. English, Arabic or both.

~15 min · 3 to 30+
🎭

Charades

Act it, describe it or hum it. Teammates turn their backs to the TV and guess. 60-second turns.

~20-30 min · 2-4 teams
👉

Most Likely To

Everyone points at once! Plus Would You Rather: run to a side of the room. Trophies at the end.

~15-25 min · 3 to 30+
🎱

Gobble Bingo

Cardboard cards and prize rounds. Auto-call is on ▶ so you can eat too.

~30-40 min · everyone

⚡ Quick fun

😎

Emoji Decode

Emojis spell out a movie, song, food or saying. Buzz in and decode it first. Over 120 puzzles.

~10-20 min · 2-4 teams
💲

The Price Is Right

Guess the price of Labubus, AirPods, Costco chicken and more. Closest without going over!

~10-20 min · 2-4 teams
❌⭕

Tic-Tac-Turkey

Trivia tic-tac-toe. Answer right to claim a square, wrong and the other team can steal.

~10-20 min · 2 teams
🦃

Pluck the Turkey

Hangman with a 10-second clock, streaks and golden letters. Lose and the turkey is NAKED 🫣

~10-20 min · any size

✅ Before guests arrive

💡 Host tips

Made a mistake? Every game has ↩️ Undo. Jeopardy's is in the ☰ menu.
Refreshed by accident? No worries. Every game saves itself and picks up where you left off.
Jeopardy reading rule: nobody answers until you finish reading the clue. The clock waits for you.
Full screen looks best on a TV: tap ⛶ in any game.
